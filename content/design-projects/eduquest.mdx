# Competition Challenge

The Lomba Desain UI/UX Identitas Manajemen Informatika 2024 challenged teams to design mobile apps that solve real Indonesian problems. Our team "Oke Gas" focused on education — specifically middle and high school students struggling with time management, motivation, and concentration.

## Design Process

- **Research** — studied the Indonesian education system, benchmarked existing learning apps, built user personas, and reviewed gamification principles for learning contexts.
- **Ideation** — sketched concepts around focus mode and time management, then layered in an achievement system and a calm blue-white gradient palette.
- **Design** — built a design system around 5 core features, mapped user flows, and produced interactive prototypes.
- **Presentation** — prepared competition materials and demo videos documenting the design decisions.

## Key Features

- **Focus Mode** — a customizable timer with app restrictions to minimize distractions during study.
- **Achievement System** — badge-based rewards and visual milestones for motivation.
- **Interactive Learning** — video explanations, practice exercises, and quizzes.
- **Social Integration** — friend connections, discussion forums, and collaborative learning.
- **Progress Tracking** — statistics on daily study duration and material completion.

## Result

The education-focused concept and comprehensive feature set resonated with the judges, and our team advanced to the grand final — demonstrating how thoughtful mobile design can address real educational-access challenges in Indonesia.
