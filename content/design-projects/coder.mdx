## Work Overview

As Publication, Design, and Documentation Coordinator at Coder Institute (since February 2025), I coordinate publication workflows, design standards, and documentation systems across the organization's educational and community content — working with developers, designers, educators, and community managers to keep everything consistent and on schedule.

## The Coordination Challenge

When I started, the organization had fragmented publication processes, an inconsistent visual language, documentation gaps, and no standardized review step — each team worked to its own standard.

## What I Do

- **Publication management** — run workflows from concept to delivery, coordinate timelines across projects, and hold quality standards for everything published.
- **Design coordination** — maintain visual consistency, manage templates and asset libraries, and review materials for brand and accessibility compliance.
- **Documentation systems** — develop documentation standards and frameworks, keep technical docs accurate with the dev team, and structure them for both technical and non-technical readers.

## Systems Implemented

- **Standardized publication workflow** — a centralized content calendar, clear phase ownership, briefing templates, and a multi-stage review process with quality checkpoints before publishing.
- **Design system & brand management** — brand guidelines, scalable templates, approval workflows with version control, and an organized digital asset library with naming conventions.
- **Documentation framework** — a documentation style guide, per-type templates, a centralized knowledge base, and onboarding docs with maintenance schedules.

## Key Projects

- **Educational content** — coordinated design and publication of 25+ educational pieces with a consistent visual language and subject-matter-expert accuracy checks.
- **Community publications** — templates and campaigns for announcements, events, and social content.
- **Technical documentation overhaul** — restructured docs for accessibility and accuracy in collaboration with development.
- **Brand standardization** — unified brand application guidelines and compliance monitoring across all materials.

## Tools

Figma (design collaboration and templates), Adobe Creative Suite, Canva, Notion (project management and docs), and Google Workspace, with Markdown and Git for documentation version control.

## Impact

- 40% faster publication turnaround through standardized workflows.
- 95% adherence to style guidelines, with a unified design system across 15+ educational materials.
- Streamlined cross-team communication and scalable systems built to survive leadership transitions.
