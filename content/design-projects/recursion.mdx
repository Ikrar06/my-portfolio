## Event Overview

RECURSION 1.0 was the first national-scale technology competition and seminar organized by Teknik Informatika Universitas Hasanuddin, running for 8 months (September 2024 – April 2025). It featured four competitions — UI/UX Design, Capture The Flag (CTF), ICT Business Plan, and Competitive Programming — culminating in the main event on 13 April 2025 with an awarding ceremony and national seminar. As Publication, Design, and Documentation Coordinator, I ran the full content pipeline from concept and design through documentation of every competition and the main event.

## Content Strategy

The approach was "Systematic Innovation" — a structured yet flexible framework that kept quality and brand identity consistent across four competitions and the seminar while adapting to each format. It emphasized cross-divisional collaboration and data-driven optimization to maximize reach across Indonesian universities.

## Deliverables

- **Brand identity** — the RECURSION 1.0 logo and variations, event mascot, and brand guidelines.
- **Merchandise** — event t-shirts, stickers, and badges.
- **Event materials** — competition and seminar posters, plus presentation templates for speakers and organizers.
- **Social media & video** — Instagram feed, Stories, and Reels templates; promotional videos, competition highlights, and motion graphics.

## Documentation Across 8 Months

- **Planning (Sep–Nov 2024)** — brand identity creation and content-calendar development.
- **Pre-event (Dec 2024–Feb 2025)** — promotional production across all categories and the registration campaign.
- **Competition (Mar–early Apr 2025)** — coverage and real-time updates across all four competitions.
- **Main event (13 Apr 2025)** — awarding ceremony, national seminar sessions, and networking.
- **Post-event** — a summary report, content-performance analysis, and an organized archive with metadata.

## Impact

- Coordinated a complete content ecosystem for the university's first national-scale event, setting new standards for event coordination.
- Promoted four competitions and educational seminars to universities nationwide.
- Established scalable workflows and a comprehensive archive that continue to guide how Recursion UH runs national events.

The project sharpened my skills in large-scale coordination, cross-divisional communication, timeline management, and analytics-driven content strategy across a diverse team of writers, designers, and photographers.
