## Work Overview

As the newly appointed Head of Creative Media for GDG on Campus Hasanuddin University (from August 2025), I lead the creative direction for our student tech community. This is the start of a new leadership term, so my focus is building the foundation: a consistent visual identity, creative workflows, and the first wave of content that sets our digital tone.

## Building From the Ground Up

Starting fresh means assessing existing brand assets, forming a newly recruited creative team, and establishing standards and systems that can scale with the community. My initial responsibilities span three areas:

- **Setup & foundation** — define the creative vision, set up design and collaboration workflows, and draft initial brand guidelines.
- **Early content** — design social media templates, the new-leadership announcement, and core-team recruitment materials.
- **Team building** — onboard new creative members, establish communication channels, and set creative standards.

## Early Work

- **Leadership announcement campaign** — visual identity and announcement posts revealing the new Lead and Co-Lead, establishing a consistent language for future announcements.
- **Core team recruitment drive** — a multi-slide recruitment campaign with a cohesive visual system.
- **Brand foundation** — researching GDG's global guidelines for local adaptation, building starter templates, and setting up asset libraries.

## Tools & Workflow

Figma for collaborative design, Canva for quick and accessible content, Adobe Photoshop for advanced editing, and Google Workspace for team coordination — with content approval, asset organization, and publishing workflows being established now.

## Goals

- **Near-term** — finish recruitment, ship a basic brand guideline, and set up a sustainable content workflow.
- **Medium-term** — grow a full visual identity system and a content library that drives engagement.
- **Long-term** — position GDG on Campus Unhas as a recognizable, professional tech community with systems that outlast leadership transitions.
